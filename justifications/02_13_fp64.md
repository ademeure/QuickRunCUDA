## CATALOG CORRECTION (refined 2026-04-23)

Catalog L446 claim: `fma.rn.f64 → DFMA → 0.05 warp-inst/SM/cy = 1.6 DFMAs/SM/cy = 475 GFLOPS FMA chip-wide (≈ 950 FLOPS)`

**Re-reading carefully**: "475 GFLOPS FMA" likely means "475 G **FMA-ops**/s" (= 475 × 2 FLOPS/FMA = 950 GFLOPS as the parenthetical clarifies). Under this interpretation:
- Catalog 950 GFLOPS = 0.95 TFLOPS
- My measured = 1.06 TFLOPS
- **Discrepancy: 12%** (not 2.2× as I initially over-claimed)

The 12% is consistent with:
- Clock: catalog at 1920 MHz; mine at ~1942 MHz DVFS (= 1.1% gap)
- Rate: catalog 0.05 vs my 0.06 warp-inst/SM/cy (= 20% gap, which propagates to throughput)
- Cross-ref to §16 catalog L1147: "DFMA at 0.95 TFLOPS = 1/76× FFMA" — internally consistent at 0.95 TF

So catalog DFMA chip TFLOPS is approximately right (0.95 vs my 1.06 = 12% off), the wording "475 GFLOPS FMA" was just confusing notation (= 475 G FMA-ops/s = 950 GFLOPS).

**Real correction needed**: catalog L446 rate "0.05 warp-inst/SM/cy" is slightly under-stated; real is 0.06 (99.95% pipe_fp64 peak).
